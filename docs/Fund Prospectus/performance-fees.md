# Performance & Fees

## Fee Structure

### Management Fee
- **Rate**: 2.0% annually
- **Calculation**: Based on net asset value
- **Payment**: Quarterly in advance
- **Purpose**: Covers operational expenses, technology costs, and management

### Performance Fee
- **Rate**: 20% of net profits
- **High Water Mark**: Performance fees only charged on new profits above previous high
- **Calculation**: Annual performance fee calculated on December 31st
- **Payment**: Annually, within 30 days of year-end

### Expense Reimbursement
- **Administrative Expenses**: Fund pays for legal, audit, and administrative costs
- **Technology Costs**: AI infrastructure and data costs
- **Trading Costs**: Brokerage commissions and market impact
- **Estimated Annual Expenses**: 0.5-1.0% of net assets

## Performance Targets

### Return Objectives
- **Target Annual Return**: 15-20%
- **Alpha Target**: 5-8% over S&P 500
- **Maximum Drawdown**: 15%
- **Sharpe Ratio Target**: > 1.5
- **Volatility Target**: 12-18% annualized

### Performance Measurement
- **Benchmark**: S&P 500 Index
- **Calculation Method**: Net of all fees and expenses
- **Reporting**: Monthly performance reports
- **Audit**: Annual audited financial statements

## Fee Examples

### Example 1: Positive Performance
- **Initial Investment**: $1,000,000
- **Year 1 Return**: 20%
- **Year 1 Value**: $1,200,000
- **Management Fee**: $20,000 (2% of average NAV)
- **Performance Fee**: $40,000 (20% of $200,000 profit)
- **Net Return**: 18% (after all fees)

### Example 2: Negative Performance
- **Initial Investment**: $1,000,000
- **Year 1 Return**: -10%
- **Year 1 Value**: $900,000
- **Management Fee**: $19,000 (2% of average NAV)
- **Performance Fee**: $0 (no performance fee on losses)
- **Net Return**: -11.9% (after management fee)

## Fee Transparency

All fees and expenses are clearly disclosed in:
- Fund offering documents
- Monthly investor reports
- Annual audited financial statements
- Quarterly investor letters

Investors receive detailed fee breakdowns with each statement.
