# Compliance

## Regulatory Framework

### Investment Adviser Act of 1940
- **Registration**: SEC registered investment adviser
- **Fiduciary Duty**: Highest standard of care for client interests
- **Compliance Program**: Comprehensive policies and procedures
- **Record Keeping**: Detailed records of all activities

### Securities Laws
- **Securities Act of 1933**: Private placement exemptions
- **Securities Exchange Act of 1934**: Trading and reporting requirements
- **Investment Company Act of 1940**: Exemptions for private funds
- **State Laws**: Compliance with applicable state regulations
