# Risk Factors

## Investment Risks

### Market Risks
- **Market Volatility**: AI systems may not perform in all market conditions
- **Sector Concentration**: Focus on AI-related companies may increase sector risk
- **Technology Disruption**: Rapid technological change may render current theses obsolete
- **Regulatory Changes**: New regulations may impact AI companies or trading strategies

### Technology Risks
- **AI Model Limitations**: Potential for model errors, biases, or overfitting
- **Data Quality**: Dependence on accurate and timely market data
- **System Reliability**: Technical failures could impact performance
- **Model Risk**: AI models may fail to adapt to changing market conditions

### Operational Risks
- **Key Personnel**: Dependence on core team for system development and management
- **Execution Risk**: Gap between AI recommendations and actual performance
- **Technology Evolution**: Risk that AI capabilities become commoditized
- **Competition**: Other firms developing similar AI-driven strategies

### Liquidity Risks
- **Market Liquidity**: Some positions may be difficult to liquidate quickly
- **Concentration Risk**: Large positions in individual securities
- **Redemption Risk**: Potential for redemption gates during market stress

## Risk Mitigation

### Technology Safeguards
- **Redundant Systems**: Multiple backup systems and fail-safes
- **Human Oversight**: All trades require final human approval
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Real-time system health monitoring
- **Regular Testing**: Comprehensive backtesting and stress testing

### Portfolio Management
- **Diversification**: Broad sector and geographic diversification
- **Position Limits**: Strict limits on individual position sizes
- **Stop Losses**: Pre-defined exit criteria for all positions
- **Liquidity Management**: Focus on liquid securities with adequate trading volume

### Operational Controls
- **Experienced Team**: Seasoned investment professionals with AI expertise
- **Robust Infrastructure**: Enterprise-grade technology and security
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Full compliance with all applicable regulations
- **Transparent Reporting**: Regular performance and risk reporting to investors

## Risk Monitoring

The fund employs sophisticated risk monitoring systems that track:
- Portfolio-level risk metrics (VaR, beta, correlation)
- Individual position risk factors
- Market and sector concentration
- Liquidity and execution risk
- Technology system health

Risk reports are generated daily and reviewed by the investment team and risk management committee.
