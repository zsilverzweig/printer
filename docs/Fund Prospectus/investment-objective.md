# Investment Objective & Strategy

## Investment Objective

The Printer AI Investment Fund seeks to generate superior risk-adjusted returns by systematically identifying and investing in companies positioned to benefit from the artificial intelligence revolution, while avoiding those likely to be disrupted.

## Target Returns

- **Primary Objective**: 15-20% annual returns
- **Alpha Target**: 5-8% annual alpha over S&P 500
- **Risk Target**: Maximum 15% drawdown, Sharpe ratio > 1.5
- **Volatility Target**: 12-18% annualized volatility

## Investment Strategy

### Core Thesis
AI adoption will create winners and losers across all sectors. Companies that successfully integrate AI into their operations will dramatically outperform those that don't.

### Systematic Approach
- **Multi-Agent AI System**: Specialized AI agents analyze different aspects of each company
- **Thesis-Driven**: Every position requires clear thesis + catalyst + time horizon (6-18 months)
- **Adversarial Testing**: Built-in stress testing and counterpoint analysis
- **Continuous Monitoring**: 24/7 surveillance of investment theses

### Investment Process
1. **Sector Screening**: AI agents identify companies with significant AI exposure
2. **Fundamental Analysis**: Deep dive into business fundamentals and AI integration
3. **Thesis Development**: Clear investment thesis with identified catalysts
4. **Risk Assessment**: Comprehensive risk analysis and mitigation strategies
5. **Position Sizing**: Risk-adjusted position sizing based on conviction level
6. **Monitoring**: Continuous thesis validation and exit criteria monitoring

## Portfolio Construction

### Diversification
- **Sector Diversification**: No more than 25% in any single sector
- **Position Sizing**: Individual positions typically 1-5% of portfolio
- **Geographic Focus**: Primarily US markets with selective international exposure
- **Market Cap Range**: Focus on mid to large-cap companies ($1B+ market cap)

### Risk Management
- **Stop Losses**: Pre-defined exit criteria for all positions
- **Position Limits**: Maximum 10% in any single position
- **Sector Limits**: Maximum 25% exposure to any single sector
- **Liquidity Requirements**: Minimum daily trading volume requirements

## Benchmark

The fund targets outperformance relative to the S&P 500 Index, with a focus on generating alpha through superior stock selection rather than market timing or leverage.
