# Financial Projections

## Revenue Model

### Fee Structure

- **Management Fee**: 1.5% annually on assets under management
- **Performance Fee**: 20% of net profits above high-water mark
- **Minimum Investment**: $1M for institutional clients, $100K for accredited investors

### Revenue Streams

1. **Core Portfolio Management**: Primary revenue from AUM fees
2. **AI Research Services**: Licensing of research capabilities to other firms
3. **Technology Platform**: SaaS model for AI investment tools
4. **Consulting Services**: Custom AI implementation for institutional clients

## Growth Projections

- **AUM**: $10M - $25M
- **Revenue**: $150K - $375K (management fees only)
- **Clients**: 5-10 institutional investors
- **Team**: 3-5 core team members

### Year 2 Targets

- **AUM**: $50M - $100M

### Year 5 Targets

- **Clients**: 100+ institutional investors

- **Technology Team**: $1M - $8M annually (Year 3-5)

### Operational Costs

- **Office Space**: $50K - $500K annually
- **Legal & Compliance**: $100K - $500K annually

## Profitability Analysis

### Break-Even Analysis

- **Break-Even AUM**: $15M - $20M
- **Break-Even Timeline**: 12-18 months
- **Key Drivers**: Management fee revenue vs. fixed costs

### Margin Progression

- **Year 1**: -50% to -20% (investment phase)
- **Year 2**: -10% to +20% (approaching profitability)
- **Year 3**: +30% to +50% (profitable growth)
- **Year 5**: +40% to +60% (mature operations)
