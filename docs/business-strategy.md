# FAQ

## What is Printer?

**Printer is a tool that helps you print money.**

It makes you smarter, by orchestrating teams of agents that help you make the best decisions possible. It's leveraged AI, with memory, built to help you get the best out of your ideas and print money.

## How does Printer work?

**Portfolios are created and then can be tracked.**

1. **You share your investment ideas** - themes, companies, or market views you want to explore
2. **AI agents create portfolios** - specialized teams research, analyze, and construct investment portfolios
3. **You track performance** - monitor how well your portfolios perform over time
4. **You follow what works** - identify and follow the strategies that generate the best returns

## Why does Printer work?

**Intelligent Orchestration**

- Teams of specialized AI agents work together on your behalf
- Each agent has specific expertise: research, analysis, risk assessment, portfolio construction
- Agents coordinate to ensure every recommendation serves your financial goals

**Memory & Learning**

- The system remembers what works and what doesn't
- Every decision improves future recommendations
- Your preferences and constraints are built into every analysis

**Money Printing Focus**

- Every recommendation is designed to help you generate returns
- Risk management is tailored to your comfort level and timeline
- Performance is measured against your ability to print money consistently
- The question isn't "did we beat the market?" but "did we help you print money?"

## What's the result?

Better investment decisions that help you print money, with less effort and more confidence.
