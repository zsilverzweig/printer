# Business Strategy

## Executive Summary

### 1. Vision

- Build an AI research engine that produces actionable, company-level investment theses.
- Start with a core top-down thesis: AI changes everything. The companies that are quicker to adopt AI will dramatically outperform the other companies in their sector.
- Printer can build a diversified portfolio that largely maps to the S&P, but selects stocks based on the winners and losers of the AI arms race, based on public messages, product pipelines, sentiment analysis and other publicly available information.
- Printer leverages AI to create a deep, virtual, 24/7, research organization that allows this thesis to be applied in real-time across a huge number of portfolio companies.
- Printer is also an experiment. We are exploring the use of AI in investment management because we know that, eventually, AI will be the superior approach. We would like to be the first to realize the gains from this.

⸻

### 2. Investment Process

Our approach combines systematic rigor with human oversight to create a robust investment process that avoids common AI pitfalls like bias and model drift.

Similar to how modern software development uses AI agents for code generation, we apply AI agents to investment research and decision-making, creating a scalable system that can analyze hundreds of companies simultaneously.

**Core Portfolio Management**

1. Every investment requires a clear thesis, identified catalyst, and defined time horizon (6-18 months)
2. Each position has explicit entry and exit criteria established upfront
3. AI agents conduct regular deep-dive analysis, producing comprehensive research reports
4. All trades are executed manually after final human review and approval

**Experimental Strategies**

1. AI-driven daily sentiment analysis for short-term trading opportunities
2. Automated end-of-day position management for experimental strategies

⸻

### 3. Operating Model

#### 3.1. High-Level Scoping

- Agents sweep sectors for AI exposure.
- Classify companies as AI-positive, AI-negative, or neutral.
- Build a living map of the AI impact landscape.

#### 3.2. Company Research Units (CRUs)

Each company has a mini-agent team that runs the Printer Decision Cycle:

- **Business Fundamentals** – revenue streams, margin impact.
- **Product / Pipeline** – AI-related initiatives, adoption timelines.
- **Management & Strategy** – credibility of AI investments.
- **Narrative** – investor sentiment, analyst chatter.
- **Risk** – structural vulnerabilities, regulatory exposure.
- **Counterpoint** – stress-tests the bull case.

Output: A Company Dossier with thesis, catalysts, risks, confidence, and kill switches.

#### 3.3. Senior Management Layer

- **Global Risk Manager** – checks concentration, cross-sector exposures.
- **Global Narrative Manager** – maps system-wide stories (e.g., "chips bottleneck").
- **Macro Manager** – adjusts for policy, rates, geopolitics.
- **Portfolio Synthesizer (PM)** – decides final allocations.

⸻

### 4. Deliverables

- **Company Dossiers** – structured research documents for each name.
- **AI Landscape Map** – overview of sector winners/losers.
- **Portfolio Actions** – adds/trims/exits with rationale.
- **Kill-Switch List** – pre-set triggers for exiting positions.
- **Cycle Log** – append-only record of theses, evidence, and updates.

⸻

### 5. Cadence

- **Quarterly**: Full landscape refresh.
- **Monthly**: Dossier updates for top 20 AI-exposed companies.
- **Weekly**: Each CRU runs a cycle; managers synthesize updates.
- **Event-driven**: Extra cycle on catalysts (earnings, product launches, trial readouts, regulation).

⸻

## Performance Analysis & System Evolution

### Performance Tracking

- Track all buys/sells with entry/exit rationale, thesis performance, and actual vs. predicted catalysts
- Monitor position-level returns, sector performance, and overall portfolio metrics
- Log decision quality: how often catalysts materialized, accuracy of time horizons, risk assessment effectiveness

### Performance Analysis Group

- Dedicated AI agents that analyze trading performance and system effectiveness
- Identify patterns in successful vs. failed theses and recommendations
- Propose improvements to prompting structures, decision criteria, and risk management
- Generate performance reports with actionable insights for system refinement

### Feedback Loop & System Evolution

- Every 5-10% portfolio turnover triggers a comprehensive system review
- Refine prompting structures based on performance analysis
- Update decision criteria and risk parameters based on what's working
- Evolve the AI agents' reasoning patterns to capture more value
- Maintain audit trail of all system changes and their impact on performance

## Edge

### Competitive Advantages

- Not competing on speed of data, but on breadth and depth of structured reasoning + continuity
- Every thesis is transparent, adversarially tested, and auditable
- Scaling edge comes from being able to run parallel company deep dives with consistent quality

### Continuous Improvement

- System gets smarter with every trade through performance analysis and prompt refinement
- Network effects from each analysis improving future decision quality
- Systematic approach to capturing and applying lessons learned
